# The reference directives say what they do

## Why

`from the last title` was read wherever "last title" stood in a line, and
several names said what a page is (`scripture`, `prosimetrum`, `Old
English verse`), not what its references do.

## Decisions (asked)

- `from the last title` → `by innermost title alone`, `no author` → `by
  title alone`, `scripture` → `by chapter and verse`, `parts in roman` →
  `part titles in roman`, `prosimetrum` → `prose by paragraph, verse by
  line`, `Old English verse` → `by half-line`; `by book, chapter, and
  paragraph` kept.
- Every directive the whole of its line; a retired name reads nothing and
  is told on open as no directive.
- The conventions' fields follow the names; the Help, the glossary, the
  steps and the one-offs' outputs say the new ones.
- The backup by import, not by reading both names: one-offs/directiveNames
  wrote 19 lines in 14 entries (De Doctrina Christiana's kept its name).

- The review's finding that the one-off reports only the lines it renamed,
  not one the old reader took with words beside "last title": refused, the
