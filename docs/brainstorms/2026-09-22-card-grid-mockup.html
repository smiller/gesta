Finding Verity

tu mi fai rimembrar dove e qual era
Proserpina nel tempo che perdette
la madre lei, ed ella primavera

You make me recall where and what Proserpina was
at the time her mother lost her, and she the spring.

quand' una donna apparve, santa e presta
When a lady appeared, holy and alert.

'Ware Snakes of Tenedos

Remember, when the self-criticizing Dragon comes knocking, full of frustration and bile and seductive darkness and apparently ineluctable despair and free-fall

ardentisque oculos suffecti sanguine et igni
sibila lambebant linguis vibrantibus ora

I can always, instead, step back, laugh it off, and walk away. Remember I can always walk out of the darkness.

Mary Oliver, When I Am Among the Trees

Around me the trees stir in their leaves
and call out, "Stay awhile."

The light flows from their branches.
And they call again, "It's simple," they say,
"and you too have come
into the world to do this, to go easy, to be filled
with light, and to shine."

O brother! Don't come without a tambourine,
as the sad can't join in God's celebration.

—Rumi, not Caia (pers. comm.),
though some days it feels like it could go either way.

Retrace our steps, escape above,
That is the task and that the strife.
(So said the Sibyl: she should know.)
How do we mend our work and life?
And how should we prepare to be
A vessel fit for amplitude?
Fulfilling hearts with joy and love
With kindness, patience, gratitude?

With that deep smile beneath it all
Shining with love, that far from strife
Moves the sun and the other stars
And lifts our wild and precious life.

The Twelfth Doctor's Farewell

Never be cruel, never be cowardly

Hate is foolish, and love is wise

Always try to be nice, but never fail to be kind

Laugh hard, run fast, be kind

Doctor, I let you go.

You have a lovely smile

so many of the hospital people came to see
Caia's lovely smile or hear her laugh.

Caia wheeled off to her liver transplant and a nurse
had tied white surgical tape as a ribbon in her hair

19 December 2007

Just saw this year's Handel's Messiah.

And if anyone were to ask me any question,
my answer would simply be "love".

Or "Caia". Same thing, really.

George Saunders: the most loving, generous and unafraid

Be a good and proactive and even somewhat desperate patient on your own behalf – seek out the most efficacious anti-selfishness medicines, energetically, for the rest of your life. Find out what makes you kinder, what opens you up and brings out the most loving, generous, and unafraid version of you – and go after those things as if nothing else matters.

Because, actually, nothing else does.
